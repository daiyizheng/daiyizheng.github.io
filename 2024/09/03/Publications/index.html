Yizheng Dai

I recently earned my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from Zhejiang University in June 2024, under the supervision of Professors Huajun Chen, Ningyu Zhang and Yizheng Dai.

My research primarily focuses on AI4Bioinformatics. I am interested in using large language models and graph neural networks to analyze biomedical data, e.g. molecules, proteins, and single-cell data. Feel free to reach me if you are interested in my research!
